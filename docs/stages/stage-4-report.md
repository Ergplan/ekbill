# Stage 4 — Wire: profile and setup

**Status: built and tested in the build container; the independent review runs on the VM.** The setup and Aap screens are wired to the repositories; every write is the owner's. The watcher is green on every step up to `rules:check`, which stays red on the unverified count until `npm run rules:verify` runs on the VM (Stage 3's open item); the steps after it were run one by one and are green.

## What exists

**Data model** (`packages/db/src/schema.ts` → `migrations/0003_profile.sql`): `businesses` gains `pincode`, `email`, `pan`, `turnover_band` (40l | 40l-1.5cr | 1.5-5cr | 5cr+; `turnover_paise` is its upper bound for the engine), `composition_legend`, `bank_name`, `bank_account`, `bank_ifsc`, `bank_holder`, `default_payment_terms_days`, `bill_design`, `bill_paper`, `bill_share_as`, `bill_accent`, `logo_file_id`, `signature_file_id`, `default_series_id`, `voice_on_arrival`, `setup_done_at`, with check constraints on every enum and format (PAN, PIN code, hex accent). `business_files` (kind logo | signature, mime png | jpeg | webp, `bytes` bytea, `size` ≤ 300 KB by constraint, `sha256`): bytes stay in Postgres; `apply_tenancy` plus an audit trigger that records the row without its bytes and an immutability trigger (a file is written once; a new logo is a new row the business points at).

**Checks (engine)** `packages/engine/src/profile.ts#checkProfile` (the MVP's `profile.ts`, `aap.ts`, `bill-look.ts` salvaged and re-shaped): PAN format; GSTIN checksum; the PAN inside the GSTIN equals the PAN typed; the GSTIN's state equals the chosen state; the setup answer maps to the registration type (Haan → regular, Composition → composition, Nahi → not-registered, Pata nahi → not-sure) and a GSTIN with "Nahi" is a contradiction; composition requires the legend (from the pack, `compositionRule(today).legend`); `e_invoicing_applies` must be answered when the band is "5 crore se zyada"; phone, UPI, email, PIN code, IFSC, account number, payment terms, the look's enums. Every refusal names the field and a dictionary key. `packages/engine/src/files.ts`: `FILE_LIMITS` (logo 300 KB, signature 60 KB, images only), `parseDataUrl`, `acceptFile`.

**Signature and logo** `packages/ui/src/trim.ts#alphaBounds` (pure: the box around the strokes with an 8-px margin) and `client.tsx#trimmed`: the pad exports a transparent PNG trimmed to the strokes and scaled down by steps until it is under 60 KB. The logo comes through the Stage 1 doors (`FileDoor` → `readPicked` at 512 px). Both are stored by `repos/files.ts#putFile` (sha256, size, mime; the engine's limits hold on the server too) and pointed at by `businesses.setFile`; `GET /api/files/[id]` serves the bytes to a member of the business. The paper prints the logo and, under "For <legal name>", the signature as the authorised signatory (`InvoicePaper`, `PaperData.logoUrl` / `signature`).

**Repositories** `repos/businesses.ts`: `getProfile`, `saveProfile` (the whole profile through `checkProfile`, marks the setup done), `setLook`, `setFile`, `setDefaultSeries`, `createBusiness` (now takes the profile and makes the two default series, `INV-` and `CN-`); `repos/files.ts`: `putFile`, `getFile`, `listFiles`; `repos/series.ts` (Stage 3) does add, rename, retire and the next number; `auth.ts#adoptBusiness` points a brand-new owner's session at the business the setup made. `allowedRoles`: every write owner-only; the generated roles test holds 60 cases.

**Actions** `apps/web/actions/business.ts`: `finishSetup` (a brand-new owner: create the business and adopt it; an owner with one: save), `saveProfile`, `saveLook`, `setBusinessFile`, `addSeries`, `changeSeries`, `chooseDefaultSeries`; inputs in `actions/schema.ts` with the table each field lands in (`docs/FIELD-TRACE.md`: 44 input fields, all owned).

**Screens** `screens/business.ts#loadBusiness` reads the profile, series and people once per request; `screens/viewer.ts` gives every real session its real business (shaped like the fixture so the screens that still read fixture bills and customers find them by seed key); `?as=` looks stay on the fixtures. Setup: the city step also takes the state (prefilled and locked from the GSTIN); the review shows the state, the IRN answer and the composition legend; "Chalo, shuru karte hain" calls `finishSetup`, lands on the field's step when refused, stores the drawn signature, and goes Home (which shows the first-bill welcome for a business with nothing in it yet). Aap: the dukaan, paise and look rows open sheets; one sticky Save sends the whole profile once ("Kuch badla nahi" when nothing changed; "Save ho gaya" and disabled after); the look saves as it is chosen; the logo and signature save as they are picked or drawn; the series sheet adds, renames, retires and sets the default; theme and language stay on the user (Stage 2); Bolke is part of the profile. Staff and the accountant see the business read-only.

**Pack correction**: three figures in the Stage 3 rule pack were ten times too large (`e-invoice-threshold-2023`, `irn-30-day-2025`, the HSN band bound said ₹50 crore, ₹100 crore and ₹50 crore for ₹5 crore, ₹10 crore and ₹5 crore). Fixed with the fixtures (`tests/unit/engine-rules.test.ts`, `engine-assess.test.ts`); the sources and phrases are unchanged. The assessment now lets the owner's "Nahi" to e-invoicing stand (a notice, never a block), as the MVP did and the setup promises; the band decides only when there is no answer.
