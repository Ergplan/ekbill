Every salvaged module names its MVP origin (tag `mvp-final`) in its header.

**Database (I2, I3)** `packages/db/src/schema.ts` → `migrations/0002_documents.sql`: the seller columns on `businesses` (`registration_type`, `gstin`, `state_code`, `turnover_paise`, `e_invoicing_applies`, `address`, `city`, `phone`, `upi`), `series` (business, name, kind bill|credit_note, prefix, padding, include_fy, next_number, fy, active), `documents` (series, kind tax_invoice|bill_of_supply|credit_note, status draft|issued|cancelled, number, financial_year, issue_date, due_date, customer_id, customer_name, customer_gstin, customer_state, customer_address, customer_phone, place_of_supply_state, flags, lines, totals, issued_snapshot, issued_at, linked_document_id, irn, cancelled_at, cancelled_reason, notes, round_to_rupee, gst_on, revision, created_by, updated_at). Check constraints hold the enums and the shape of each status (a draft has no number; an issued row has number, year, time and snapshot; cancelled ⇔ cancelled_at). `apply_tenancy` on both tables (RLS, audit, no delete, no model write). Two triggers on `documents`: `issue_gate` and `immutable_once_issued`. `repos/series.ts` (list, get, default, create, update: the next number only goes up), `repos/documents.ts` (`createDraft`, `updateDraft`, `getDocument`, `listDocuments`, `issueDocument`, `cancelDocument`, `createCreditNote`; `allowedRoles`: drafts and issue by owner and staff, cancel and credit note by the owner). The seed gives Sharma Electricals a regular profile (Maharashtra, ₹5 lakh, e-invoicing "no"), Gupta Kirana a composition profile (Uttar Pradesh), Verma Traders nothing, and each business a bill series and a credit-note series.
